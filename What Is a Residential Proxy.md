## What Is a Residential Proxy?

A **Residential Proxy** is a proxy service that routes internet traffic through real residential networks.

Unlike data center proxies, residential proxies use real residential IP addresses assigned by Internet Service Providers (ISPs), making them closer to regular user network environments.

Because of their authenticity and stability, residential proxies are widely used in scenarios that require:

- Regional network access
- Stable connections
- Long-term operations
- Location-based workflows
